Place raw building model output files here (from BEopt, EnergyPlus, eQUEST, or custom simulations).

Supported Formats & Expected Content:
- BEopt CSV outputs (e.g. hourly building total electric power in kW or kWh)
- EnergyPlus CSV output files (e.g. eplusout.csv with Electricity:Facility)
- Custom 8760 CSV load profiles (containing datetime/hour and kW columns)

You can save individual baseline vs. proposed files or multi-column comparison files in this directory.
